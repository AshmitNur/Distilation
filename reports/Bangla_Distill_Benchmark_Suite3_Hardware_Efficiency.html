Edge & Mobile Feasibility Summary
Frontier teacher models (Gemma-2-9B and Qwen-3.5-9B) require dedicated data-center GPUs (A100 / RTX 3090/4090 with 20+ GB VRAM). In contrast, the distilled student model executes comfortably within 1.4 GB VRAM, allowing it to run smoothly on edge computing boards (Jetson Orin Nano, Raspberry Pi 5 with llama.cpp, Apple Silicon unified memory) and free-tier cloud GPUs (Google Colab / Kaggle T4).
