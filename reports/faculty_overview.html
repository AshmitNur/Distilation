Meeting Opener: The 60-Second Faculty Pitch
"Bengali has over 230 million native speakers, yet open-source language models under 1-billion parameters suffer from severe script fragmentation, unnatural grammar, and high perplexity. Deploying 7B+ models is impractical for mobile or edge environments. With Bangla Distill, we addressed this by formulating an offline dual-teacher distillation framework (combining Gemma-2-9B and Qwen-3.5-9B) to supervise an ultra-compact student model (Qwen2.5-0.5B). Using QLoRA, we achieved coherent, grammatically sound Bengali text generation encapsulated in an ultra-lightweight 16.8 MB LoRA adapter runnable on free-tier T4 GPUs (<6 GB VRAM). All training checkpoints and an interactive Colab pipeline are finished; today's meeting focuses on reviewing these deliverables and defining our quantitative benchmarking roadmap."
1 Methodology & Distillation Architecture

The project employs an Offline Knowledge Distillation + Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning (PEFT) paradigm designed to maximize linguistic density while maintaining strict compute bounds.

Dual-Teacher Strategy

Blends Gemma-2-9B-IT (deep reasoning, structured exposition) with Qwen 3.5 9B (morphological script robustness). Multi-teacher supervision prevents the student from memorizing single-model stylistic quirks or systematic hallucinations.

Student Architecture

Selected Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B (~500M parameters). Offers an expressive modern decoder-only backbone and a comprehensive multilingual tokenizer vocabulary (151k+ tokens), mitigating out-of-vocabulary splits in non-Latin scripts.

QLoRA Quantization

Freezes base weights in 4-bit NormalFloat (NF4) precision via bitsandbytes. Adapters target all 7 linear projection matrices (Attention + MLP) with rank r = 16 and scaling α = 32, producing an exceptionally small 16.8 MB weight delta.

2 Curated Bengali Training Corpus

Rather than noisy raw web crawls, the student was supervised over 80,000 curated, high-density Bengali informational articles (~46.1 MB uncompressed text in strict JSONL format).
